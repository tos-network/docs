# TOS Network Roadmap

Our development roadmap outlines the strategic direction of TOS Network, guided by our core principle of **"Don't Trust, Verify it"**. Every milestone is designed to enhance security, privacy, scalability, and usability.

## 🎯 Vision & Mission

### Vision
To become the world's leading privacy-focused blockchain platform that seamlessly integrates AI-powered mining with traditional cryptocurrency infrastructure.

### Mission
Provide a mathematically verifiable, cryptographically secure, and environmentally conscious blockchain solution that empowers users with true financial privacy and productive work opportunities.
