## 📈 Exchanges

TOS Network will be listed on mainstream centralized exchanges (CEX) and decentralized exchanges (DEX) through community governance voting. The community will decide which exchanges to prioritize based on factors such as liquidity, security, user experience, and regional accessibility.

### Exchange Listing Process

1. **Community Proposal** - Any community member can submit an exchange listing proposal
2. **Discussion Period** - 2-week community discussion and feedback
3. **Governance Vote** - Token holders vote on the proposal
4. **Implementation** - Upon approval, the team initiates exchange integration

Stay tuned for upcoming governance proposals on exchange listings.

## Community Guidelines

### Code of Conduct

1. **Be Respectful**: Treat all community members with respect
2. **Stay On Topic**: Keep discussions relevant to TOS Network
3. **No Spam**: Avoid repetitive or promotional content
4. **Help Others**: Share knowledge and assist newcomers
5. **Follow Platform Rules**: Respect each platform's specific guidelines

### Content Guidelines

- **Educational Content**: Encouraged and rewarded
- **Technical Discussions**: Welcome in appropriate channels
- **Price Discussion**: Limited to designated channels
- **Criticism**: Constructive feedback is welcome

**"Don't Trust, Verify it"** - Our community values transparency, education, and helping others understand and verify the technology behind TOS Network!
